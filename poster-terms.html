Poster & Payment Terms

Version 2026-08-03 · Effective August 3, 2026

These Poster & Payment Terms apply when you enable Poster mode on QuickGigs. They supplement the QuickGigs Terms of Service.

1. Posting and hiring

You may post local tasks (minimum $20 CAD), review applicants, and accept one Tasker per task. You are responsible for accurate listings and for confirming when work is complete.

2. Escrow

After you accept a Tasker, you fund the task through Stripe. Payment is held in escrow until you confirm completion. Funds are then released to the Tasker (or their approved guardian for teens). Chat unlocks after funding when escrow is enabled.

3. Platform fees

Fee model (CAD): one-off tasks 25% · recurring 10% · subscribers 20% one-off / 8% recurring. Recurring hourly jobs charge the fee on each period’s total.

Fees are calculated server-side and recorded on the payment. Platform fees are generally non-refundable once a task is completed and paid out.

4. Payment method verification

Before posting, you may be required to verify a payment method. Verification does not charge the task amount; escrow charges happen when you fund an accepted task.

5. Conduct
  • Do not solicit off-platform payment to avoid fees.
  • Do not request adult or sexual content — zero tolerance.
  • Treat Taskers fairly and release or dispute funds in good faith.
6. Acceptance

Enabling Poster mode and checking the consent box records your acceptance of these Poster & Payment Terms and the general Terms at the versions shown on that screen.
