Why I built SwiftGigs

I'm a software development student in Calgary. I built SwiftGigs because hiring someone local for a small job — or getting paid for one — shouldn't mean sketchy DMs or waiting on cheques. Every payment is held in escrow until the job's done. It's Canadian-owned, CAD-only, and built here in Alberta.
